Shop & Receipt

The Pradeep Sales logo is built in. It prints small, in black & white, on receipts.

Loyalty Points

When off, nothing about points appears anywhere in billing, receipts or reports. Points already given are kept and come back when you switch it on again.

Party settings (enrolled or not, points multiplier, manual adjustment) are in Customers → Edit / Points. Points need a 10-digit mobile number on the bill.

Backup, Restore & Exports

All data is stored on this tablet only. Take a backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe (Google Drive, WhatsApp to yourself, email).
